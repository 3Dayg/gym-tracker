# Boxing Conditioning plan

The app includes a ready-made **Boxing Conditioning** plan. It is built for people who box and want better round stamina, plus a bit of muscle — not a bodybuilding split.

Find it on the **Plans** tab, or start it directly from the **Workout** tab — the list shows **9 exercises · 1:00 rest**. You do not need a second timer.

## How to read the numbers

Boxing work is logged as **timed rounds** with a real duration — no reps involved:

- **Rounds** = how many rounds
- **Time** = the length of one round (3:00 for bag and shadow work, 0:45 for the plank)
- Tap **Start** on a round and let the phone count down. At 0:00 the round is ticked and a **1:00** rest starts. Tick the circle yourself only if you finish early.

Strength work is normal sets and reps. Rest **60–90 seconds** between those.

## The session

| Order | Exercise | Work | Why it's there |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jump Rope | 5 × 3 min | Footwork, warm-up, aerobic base |
| 2 | Shadow Boxing | 3 × 3 min | Technique under fatigue, shoulder endurance |
| 3 | Heavy Bag Rounds | 4 × 3 min | Punch output and fight-pace stamina |
| 4 | Push-Up | 4 × 15 | Chest, shoulders, triceps |
| 5 | Dumbbell Row | 3 × 10 | Upper back and pulling strength (add a weight you can finish cleanly) |
| 6 | Walking Lunge | 3 × 12 | Legs and hip stability; 12 per side if you prefer |
| 7 | Burpee | 3 × 12 | Conditioning that also hits the whole body |
| 8 | Russian Twist | 3 × 20 | Rotational core for punches |
| 9 | Plank | 3 × 45 sec | Midline stamina |

Expect about **50–60 minutes**. Do it **2–3 times a week** on days you are not sparring hard.

## How to progress

- **Stamina:** keep the rounds at 3 minutes, but throw more punches, skip faster, or add a 4th shadow-boxing round.
- **Muscle:** add a little weight on rows and lunges when 10–12 reps feel easy. You can also add a set of pull-ups from the exercise library.
- **Harder finish:** replace the last burpee set with battle ropes or kettlebell swings (both are in the library).

## Editing the plan

This is your plan — change anything on the Plans tab. If you delete it, the app will not recreate it automatically.
