# Boxing Conditioning plans

The app includes three ready-made boxing sessions from the practical Mike Tyson split: **Boxing Conditioning A**, **B**, and **C**. They are built for people who box and want better round stamina, plus repeatable calisthenics — not a bodybuilding split.

Find them on the **Plans** tab, or start one from the **Workout** tab. Tapping a plan opens a preview with the notes, exercise list, work time, and 1:00 rest, then **Start Workout**. You do not need a second timer.

## Weekly layout

| Day | Session | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Boxing Conditioning A | Push-ups, dips, squats, core, bag rounds |
| Tuesday | Easy roadwork | Aerobic base (not a seeded plan — run, bike, or walk on your own) |
| Wednesday | Boxing Conditioning B | Pulling, shrugs, core, sprint intervals |
| Thursday | Skill or rest | Shadowboxing, a technique class, or a full day off |
| Friday | Boxing Conditioning C | Full-body circuit and bag rounds |
| Saturday | Optional roadwork or jump rope | Low to moderate conditioning |
| Sunday | Rest | Recovery, walking, mobility |

Do **A / B / C** on non-sparring days when you can. Add roadwork only when joints and sleep are holding up.

## How to read the numbers

Boxing work is logged as **timed rounds** with a real duration — no reps involved:

- **Rounds** = how many rounds
- **Time** = the length of one round (2:00 rope on A, 3:00 bag rounds, 0:15 neck holds and sprints)
- Tap **Start** on a round and let the phone count down. At 0:00 the round is ticked and a **1:00** rest starts. Tick the circle yourself only if you finish early.

Strength work is normal sets and reps. The article’s ranges are collapsed to a recoverable starting number — add reps when the last set still has 1–3 left in reserve.

Cardio blocks (the bike warm-up on B) do not start the rest timer. Tick the block and keep moving.

|---|---|---|---|

## How to progress

- **Calisthenics:** add a few reps, not a new set, until the top of the article’s range still feels like RPE 7–8.
- **Bag and rope:** cleaner rounds before harder rounds. Keep the rest at 1:00.
- **Sprints:** add a round or a few seconds, not both in the same week.
- **Pull-ups:** use an assisted variation or lat pulldown until 8 strict reps are there.
- Deload when joints feel worse, sleep drops, or boxing skill gets sloppy for more than a week.

This is enough work for most people. The classic Tyson camp volume is history, not a weekly checklist.

## Editing the plans

These are your plans — change anything on the Plans tab. If you delete one, the app will not recreate it automatically. Existing installs that still have the old all-in-one **Boxing Conditioning** plan lose that template on the next launch and gain A, B, and C instead. Workouts already logged keep their original name in History.
