# Gym Tracker — User Guide

Gym Tracker is a fully offline workout companion. Everything you log stays on your phone — there is no account, no sync, and no internet required.

## The five tabs

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Workout** | Start and log a training session, with work and rest timers |
| **Plans** | Build reusable workout templates |
| **Exercises** | Browse the exercise library and add your own |
| **History** | Look back at every finished workout |
| **Progress** | Charts, personal records, and body weight tracking |

## Quick answers

- **Do I need a plan to work out?** No — use *Quick Start* on the Workout tab and add exercises as you go.
- **Where do I change my weight?** Tap the person icon (or Profile on the Progress tab). See [Profile](profile.md).
- **What happens if I close the app mid-workout?** Nothing is lost. The workout and any remaining work/rest countdown come back. If the countdown already finished, the timer stays off. A session left unfinished overnight asks whether to resume or discard.
- **Can I change kg to lb?** Yes — gear icon on the Workout tab, or Units on Profile. Numbers convert automatically (100 kg becomes about 220.5 lb). Switching back restores the original values.
- **Will deleting an exercise erase my history?** No. Past workouts keep the exercise name even if the exercise is gone.
