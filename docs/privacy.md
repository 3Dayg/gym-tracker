# Gym Tracker Privacy Policy

Gym Tracker does not use an account, iCloud, or the internet. Nothing you log is uploaded, sold, or shared.

This page matches the in-app Privacy screen (Profile → Privacy). Keep them in sync if you edit either.

## What stays on this iPhone

Workouts, plans, custom exercises, units, rest-timer preference, and optional height and body weight. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, they only fire on this iPhone when a work round or rest period ends. They are not sent to a server.

## What we do not collect

No location, contacts, photos, Health app access, analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports.

## Your choices

Height and body weight are optional. You can skip them on first launch and add or remove them later in Profile. You can turn off notifications in iOS Settings.
