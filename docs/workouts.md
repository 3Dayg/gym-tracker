# Logging a workout

## Starting

On the **Workout** tab you have two options:

- **Quick Start** — begins an empty workout. Add exercises as you go.
- **Start from a plan** — tap any plan below Quick Start. Strength exercises appear with sets, reps, and weight; timed exercises (plank, bag rounds) with a **duration**; cardio exercises with **time, speed, incline, and distance**.

Only one workout can be active at a time. If you close or even quit the app, the workout is still there when you return.

## Logging sets

Each exercise is a section with one row per set, round, or cardio block:

- **Strength:** tap **weight** or **reps** to adjust, then tick the circle when the set is done. The rest timer starts.
- **Timed** (plank, jump rope, bag rounds): set the duration in **minutes and seconds**, then tick the circle when the round ends. The rest timer starts — round breaks are real rests.
- **Cardio:** tap **time**, **speed**, or **incline**, then tick the circle when the block is done. **Distance** fills in automatically from speed × time; overwrite it with what the treadmill shows if you prefer. The rest timer does **not** start — keep moving.
- **Add Set** / **Add Round** / **Add Block** duplicates the last row.
- **Add Exercise** (bottom of the list) opens the exercise picker — search or filter by muscle group and tap to add.
- Swipe a row left to delete it. Deleting the last row of an exercise removes the exercise.

## The rest timer

When you complete a set, a countdown bar appears at the bottom:

- The duration comes from settings (gear icon, default 1:30).
- Tap **Skip** to dismiss it early.
- If you leave the app, a notification fires when the rest is over (allow notifications when the app asks).

## Finishing or discarding

- **Finish** (top-right) saves the workout to History. Sets you never checked off are discarded, and exercises without a single completed set are dropped — the saved session contains only what you actually did. Finish is disabled until at least one set is completed.
- **Cancel** (top-left) discards the whole workout after a confirmation. Nothing is saved.
